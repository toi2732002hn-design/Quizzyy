QUIZZY - HOC TU VUNG
====================

CAI DAT (1 lan, khoang 2-3 phut)
1. Giai nen TOAN BO file zip ra mot thu muc (chuot phai > Extract All).
2. Bam dup "CAI DAT QUIZZY.bat".
3. Cho thanh tien trinh chay den 100%. Can ket noi mang trong luc cai
   (de tai thanh phan chay ung dung). Khong can cai them gi khac.
4. Xong: bieu tuong Quizzy xuat hien tren man hinh nen. Bam dup de hoc.

LAN DAU MO
- Quizzy hoi ten hoc sinh va ten giao vien. Hai ten nay duoc ghi vao cac file Excel xuat ra.
- Khong co mat khau / ma PIN.
- Hoc phan "Vocabulary to talk about occupation" (44 tu) da co san.

DUNG HANG NGAY
- Bam dup bieu tuong Quizzy tren man hinh nen.
- Muc "Buoi hoc": tu dong luu ngay hoc, hoc phan/file da hoc, hoat dong va diem.
  Co the sua ngay hoc, ghi chu, hoac them buoi hoc ghi tay.
- Dong cua so Quizzy: ung dung tu tat sau khoang 90 giay.
  Hoac bam ten ban (goc tren ben phai) > "Thoat Quizzy".

GO CAI DAT
- Settings > Apps > Quizzy > Uninstall, hoac chay Uninstall.bat trong
  %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Quizzy

DU LIEU
- Luu tai %LOCALAPPDATA%\Quizzy\data (khong mat khi cai lai / nang cap).
- Sao luu: chep thu muc "data".

CAI KHONG CAN MANG
- Tai san file node-v22.x-win-x64.zip tu nodejs.org, doi ten thanh node-win-x64.zip
  va dat canh file "Install.ps1". (Van can mang de cai thu vien o buoc 4.)
